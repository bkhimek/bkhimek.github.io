How to read this

  • A lower value means closer to the mouse’s own pre-antibiotic composition during the window; it is not a direct measure of recovery rate.
  • All standard-diet day-0 samples were sequenced in run 1. Low-MAC day-0 samples and all analysed days 8–12 samples were sequenced in run 2.
  • Baseline sequencing run is confounded with diet, cohort concurrency is unverified, and only five cages contribute. Results are therefore reported descriptively, and the pattern cannot be attributed to diet.
