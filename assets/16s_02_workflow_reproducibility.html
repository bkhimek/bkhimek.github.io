Reproducibility: QIIME 2 against nf-core/ampliseq

The same checksummed per-sample reads were processed by nf-core/ampliseq 3.1.0 with DADA2 settings matched to QIIME 2, and the frozen analysis was rerun. The decision rule was tagged before the second workflow ran.

Figure 4

Cage values of the frozen primary measure in the two workflows.

Each point is one cage: the cage mean of the Bray–Curtis distance between each mouse’s days 8–12 samples and its own day-0 sample, computed from QIIME 2 (horizontal) and nf-core/ampliseq (vertical). STD = standard diet; PD = low-MAC diet (the paper’s MD arm). The dashed line marks equal values. The diet contrast differed by 0.0001 between workflows (−0.1454 against −0.1453), against a prespecified tolerance of 0.02, and the cage pattern was the same.
